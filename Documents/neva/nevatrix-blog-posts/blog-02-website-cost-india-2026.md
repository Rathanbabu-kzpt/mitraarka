## H2: Why Website Costs Vary So Much in India

You can find websites being sold for ₹2,000 on Fiverr and for ₹20,00,000 by a large agency. How can the same product have such a massive price range?

The answer lies in what's actually being built. A ₹2,000 website is a pre-made template with your logo dropped in — no strategy, no SEO, no customisation, no performance work. A ₹20,00,000 website from a large agency includes months of design research, custom development, user testing, and ongoing support.

Most businesses need something in between — well-built, professional, optimised, and reasonably priced. Here's how to understand what you're actually paying for.

## H2: What Factors Change Website Cost the Most?

Understanding these variables helps you have smarter conversations with any web development company.

### H3: 1. Design — Template vs. Custom
A template-based design is 40–60% cheaper than custom design. Templates work fine for most businesses — the key is whether the template is well-chosen and properly customised. Full custom design involves UI/UX research, wireframes, brand-aligned visual systems, and is worth the investment for businesses where design is a key differentiator.

### H3: 2. Number of Pages and Features
More pages = more content, more development time, more SEO work. Similarly, features like booking systems, membership portals, multi-language support, and live chat all add to the cost — because they add real development complexity.

### H3: 3. Content — Who Writes It?
Many businesses underestimate this cost. Great website copy is as important as great design — it's what converts visitors into customers. If you provide all the text, images, and content, development costs stay lower. If the agency creates content for you, expect to add ₹10,000–₹50,000+ depending on depth.

### H3: 4. SEO — Built In vs. Added On
Some agencies build websites and charge extra for SEO. At Nevatrix, on-page SEO is built into every website we create — correct heading structure, metadata, page speed, schema markup, and image optimisation are not optional extras. Ask any prospective agency whether SEO is included or charged separately.

### H3: 5. Ongoing Maintenance
Most agencies charge a monthly or annual maintenance fee (typically ₹2,000–₹10,000/month) for security updates, backups, performance monitoring, and content updates. This is genuinely valuable — don't skip it.

## H2: Red Flags When Comparing Website Quotes in India

When you get multiple quotes from web development companies in India or Warangal, watch out for:

- **Quotes with no itemised breakdown** — what exactly are you paying for?
- **No mention of mobile responsiveness** — this is non-negotiable in 2026
- **"SEO included" with no specifics** — what does that actually mean?
- **No post-launch support plan** — who do you call when something breaks?
- **Guaranteed #1 Google ranking** — nobody can promise this; walk away
- **Very low prices with no portfolio** — often results in abandoned projects or unusable websites

Always ask to see recent examples of websites built by the team you're hiring.
