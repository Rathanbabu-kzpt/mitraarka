# Mobile App Development Cost in India 2026: Full Pricing Breakdown

You have a mobile app idea. The big question on your mind: **how much will it cost?**

Getting a straight answer is surprisingly hard. Most development companies give you vague estimates, ask you to "schedule a call", or quote wildly different numbers with no explanation of why.

This guide gives you honest, detailed, current pricing for mobile app development in India in 2026 — broken down by app type, platform, features, and team structure. By the end, you'll know exactly what to budget and what to ask every developer you speak to.

---

## H2: Why Mobile App Costs Vary So Much

Before the numbers, it's worth understanding why two apps can cost ₹50,000 and ₹50,00,000 respectively.

**The factors that drive app development cost:**

1. **Platform** — Android only, iOS only, or both (cross-platform)
2. **Complexity** — number of screens, features, and user flows
3. **Backend requirements** — does the app need a server, database, or APIs?
4. **Design** — off-the-shelf vs. custom UI/UX
5. **Third-party integrations** — payment gateways, maps, social login, etc.
6. **Team location** — Indian developer rates are typically 60–80% lower than US/UK rates
7. **Agency vs. freelancer** — agencies cost more but bring process, testing, and accountability
8. **Post-launch support** — ongoing maintenance, updates, and scaling

Understanding these variables helps you get accurate quotes and avoid surprises.

## H2: Mobile App Development Cost in India — 2026 Price Guide

### H3: Simple/Basic Apps
**Cost: ₹50,000 – ₹2,00,000**
**Timeline: 6–12 weeks**

Simple apps have limited features, no complex backend, and straightforward user flows.

What's typically included:
- 5–10 screens
- Static or semi-dynamic content
- Basic user authentication (email/password login)
- Simple API integration (news feed, weather, etc.)
- No real-time features
- Single platform (Android or iOS)

**Examples:** Informational apps, simple calculators, basic service directories, static event apps.

---

### H3: Mid-Complexity Apps
**Cost: ₹2,00,000 – ₹8,00,000**
**Timeline: 3–6 months**

Mid-complexity apps have more features, custom UI, a backend server, and at least one integration with an external service.

What's typically included:
- 15–30 screens
- Custom UI/UX design
- User authentication + profiles
- Real-time push notifications
- Payment gateway integration (Razorpay, Stripe)
- Admin panel / dashboard
- Database and API backend
- Both Android and iOS (cross-platform)

**Examples:** Food ordering apps, appointment booking apps, ecommerce apps, service marketplace apps, fitness tracking apps.

---

### H3: Complex/Enterprise Apps
**Cost: ₹8,00,000 – ₹30,00,000+**
**Timeline: 6–18 months**

Complex apps involve sophisticated architecture, multiple user roles, real-time features, and often AI or ML integration.

What's typically included:
- 40+ screens across multiple user roles
- Full custom design system
- Real-time features (chat, live tracking, video calls)
- Multiple payment options + subscription billing
- Advanced backend (microservices, scalable infrastructure)
- Analytics and reporting dashboards
- CMS for admin content management
- Advanced security and compliance features
- Both Android and iOS + potentially web

**Examples:** Ride-hailing apps, telemedicine platforms, fintech apps, learning management systems, on-demand services with driver/user/admin flows.

---

### H3: AI-Powered Apps
**Cost: ₹5,00,000 – ₹25,00,000+**
**Timeline: 5–15 months**

AI-powered apps integrate machine learning or LLM capabilities for intelligent features.

What adds cost:
- Custom ML model training or LLM integration (GPT-4, Claude, Gemini)
- Natural language processing for chat interfaces
- Image recognition or computer vision features
- Recommendation engines or personalisation systems
- AI-based fraud detection or predictive features

**Examples:** AI fitness coach apps, intelligent document processing, AI-powered customer service apps, personalised learning apps.

## H2: Platform Cost Comparison — Android vs iOS vs Cross-Platform

| Platform Choice | Relative Cost | Pros | Best For |
|----------------|--------------|------|----------|
| Android only | Base price | Largest Indian user base; faster review | Testing Indian market first |
| iOS only | +10–20% | Higher spending users; less fragmentation | Premium products, global market |
| Both (native) | +80–100% | Best performance | Apps where platform-native UX matters |
| Cross-platform (React Native/Flutter) | +30–50% vs single | One codebase, both platforms | Most startups and businesses |

**For most Indian businesses:** Cross-platform development (React Native or Flutter) offers the best cost-performance balance — you get both Android and iOS from a single codebase at 50–70% the cost of building native apps for each platform.

## H2: Hidden Costs to Budget For

Many businesses are surprised by costs that weren't in the original quote. Here's what to account for:

**App Store fees:**
- Apple Developer Account: $99/year (~₹8,300/year)
- Google Play Developer Account: $25 one-time (~₹2,100)

**Backend hosting:**
- AWS / Google Cloud / Azure: ₹2,000–₹20,000/month depending on scale

**Third-party API costs:**
- Payment gateways (Razorpay): transaction-based fees
- Google Maps API: usage-based pricing
- SMS/OTP services: per-message fees
- Push notification services: volume-based

**Post-launch maintenance:**
- OS updates (iOS/Android releases break things): ₹5,000–₹20,000/update cycle
- Bug fixes and performance improvements: ₹5,000–₹15,000/month

**Marketing:**
- Getting downloads costs money — budget for app store optimisation (ASO) and paid installs

A good rule of thumb: budget 15–20% of your development cost annually for maintenance and operations.

## H2: Should You Build an MVP First?

If you're a startup or launching a new product, the answer is almost always yes. An MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is a stripped-down version of your app with only the core features needed to test your concept with real users.

**Why MVP first:**
- Validates your idea before spending the full budget
- Gets you to market 3–5x faster
- Lets you incorporate real user feedback into the full build
- Reduces risk of building the wrong thing

**MVP cost in India:** ₹1,00,000–₹4,00,000 for most service or marketplace apps.
